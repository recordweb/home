A story from everyday public-sector work

The chat stays.
The context becomes traceable.

How an existing business application can be connected to RecordWeb without requiring people to do their work twice or abandon the tools they already know.

Deutsch English

Scene 1 · The work is already happening

Ms Roth works through a question with her team.

A parliamentary question needs an answer. Ms Roth needs current figures, professional context, and wording that her team can stand behind.

Ms Roth

She is responsible for the response and brings the appropriate specialists together. Her immediate task is to deliver a correct answer on time.

The specialist team

Team members check figures, discuss uncertainty, and agree the professional interpretation. They collaborate in their familiar team chat.

The reality: The response does not emerge only in the final document. It emerges through questions, intermediate results, professional knowledge, coordination, and a decision jointly supported by the team.

Scene 2 · A familiar tool

The chat is a place where real work happens.

Ms Roth should not have to work in a new system merely so that the collaboration can later be understood and traced.

In the team chat

Ms Roth asks for the current time series. One specialist checks the figures. Another adds a methodological note. Together they agree the wording for the response.

In question management

The question-management system continues to show which question is being handled, who is responsible, and when the answer is due.

The chat and question management have different jobs. Both can retain their familiar data models and their own user interfaces.

Scene 3 · The bridge

Nobody has to replace the chat.

Source Integration creates a connection between an existing application and RecordWeb. It is not another work tool for Ms Roth, and it is not a second repository that she has to maintain manually.

The business application

The chat continues to manage the conversation. It knows which messages belong to which discussion and who participates in it.

The Source Adapter

It creates the connection. It can make selected content and information about its origin available to a RecordWeb implementation.

RecordWeb

A RecordWeb implementation can create a persistently identifiable, verifiable, and later findable Record from that information.

No big bang

The application can keep working. The connection can be introduced incrementally: first for one process, then for additional information and applications.

Scene 4 · Clear responsibility

Who does what?

Source Integration does not blur the line between business responsibility and responsibility for a Record. It makes that line visible.

The business application remains responsible

It remains responsible for its business object: for example, a conversation, a question, a case file, or a document in its own domain context.

The Source Adapter connects

It may provide content, provenance information, or both. It does not need to create a RecordWeb identifier, manage snapshots, or maintain a version history.

The Producer creates the Record

Only the Producer gives information a persistent RecordWeb identifier, checks it against the applicable rules, and creates a finalised snapshot where appropriate.

A Record is more than a copy

It preserves a specific, traceable representation: with identity, versions, integrity evidence, and a clear indication of which state is meant later.

The simple boundary: A Source Adapter can supply information. A Record exists only where a Producer creates it as a RecordWeb Record and takes responsibility for it.

Scene 5 · Not everything may be exported

Sometimes the content stays in the source system.

Not every piece of information may or should leave a protected application. This may apply to confidential, highly sensitive, or classified information.

The protected content

The full content remains in the source system. Its access rights, protection requirements, and business responsibilities continue to apply there.

The traceable reference

The Source Adapter can still identify the originating system, the object identifier, the time of capture, and the business context to which a reference relates.

The basis for trust

In this situation, a reference does not replace a retained copy. Trust rests on traceable responsibility and on verifiable statements about how the participating application is governed and operated.

A deliberate choice

This is not an incomplete integration. It is an appropriate approach when protection and confidentiality matter more than transferring the complete content.

Scene 6 · From notice to verified reference

A notification is not yet evidence.

Once the Producer has created a Record, it can notify the answer-management system that a new state is available. That is useful, but it does not replace verification.

SoX reports a new state

A message may say that the chat has been captured or that a Record has been finalised. It can trigger an interface refresh or a new attempt to continue the process.

Answer management verifies for itself

Before a case is finally closed, the answer-management system resolves the RecordWeb identifier and checks the identified, finalised snapshot.

The fixed reference

Only then does the CaseRecord refer to that specific state. This makes it possible to determine later which version was actually used in the case.

The consequence

A callback or status message can make work easier. Verification of the target Record remains the responsibility of the system that finalises the reference.

Important: Evidence is not a claim in a message. Evidence comes from verifying the specific, finalised state of the Record.

Scene 7 · The story as a flow

From collaboration to traceable context.

1. Work

A business object is created or changed in a business application: for example, a conversation, a document, or a question.

2. Connection

A Source Adapter makes selected content, provenance information, or both available to a RecordWeb implementation.

3. Record

A Producer creates a Record where appropriate, verifies it, gives it a persistent identity, and finalises a particular state.

4. Use

Other applications can later find and verify the Record, and refer to a specifically identified, finalised state.

Outlook

Existing applications become connectable.

Source Integration does not mandate a particular technical mechanism. It can be implemented through an API, an event, a file exchange, a queue, or another agreed approach.

The business application remains strong in its own domain.

The Source Adapter creates the connection.

The Producer takes responsibility for the Record.

Step by step, this creates a network of traceable relationships.
