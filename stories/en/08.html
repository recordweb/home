RecordWeb · Perspective of a language model

I need context.

What I, as an LLM, can do better when information is not merely stored, but linked as records.

I can compose text. But I can only help responsibly if I know what it refers to, who is accountable for it, and which version applies.
This presentation deliberately speaks in the first-person perspective of an LLM. It does not promise autonomous administration or error-free AI. It makes a simple case: good AI support does not depend only on capable models. It depends decisively on accessible, complete, and traceable context. Important: it was generated by an LLM with access to RecordWeb itself, and is presented here unedited.

Scene 1 · A simple request

"Help me draft the answer to the parliamentary question."

This is a task where I can quickly appear helpful. I can summarise information, phrase variants, flag contradictions, and suggest clarifying questions.

But before I answer, I need something decisive: the right context.

The opening should not be technology-centred. The question is familiar: can AI help with writing, checking, or summarising? Yes — but the quality of the answer depends on whether the context is reliably available.

Scene 2 · Today's information space

Without context, I see fragments.

A dossier may contain important documents. In real work, however, relevant information often sits additionally in specialist applications, chats, emails, working files, and different versions.

I can certainly process several documents. The problem isn't volume alone. Without explicit relationships, I don't reliably know: does this chat message belong to exactly this question? Is the draft outdated? Which figure is approved? Is this email relevant, or just a discarded interim opinion?

Scene 3 · Credible language is not enough

When context is missing, I have to guess — or I should ask.

What I don't know without reliable context

  • Which version is binding?
  • Which source is current and authoritative?
  • Which statements were corrected or discarded?
  • Who is allowed to approve the answer?
  • Whether the documents are complete

What can happen then

  • I use an outdated draft
  • I mix up different cases
  • I treat an informal statement as binding
  • I sound convincing, but without solid grounding
  • I create more work for human review
An LLM can mask uncertainty well in its language. That's why verifiable context matters more for responsible augmentation than an especially smooth formulation.
This slide is central to credibility. Do not claim RecordWeb prevents hallucination. A language model can make mistakes even with good context. But RecordWeb makes the basis, provenance, and limits of an AI-supported answer more verifiable, and makes it easier for humans to exercise professional control.

Scene 4 · A different information space

With RecordWeb, I can work along relationships.

I don't just receive files. I can use a traceable relationship between the question, the case, professional collaboration, versions, responsibility, and the answer.

I don't just see content. I can also see how content relates to other content.
Here, don't claim that an AI automatically gets access to everything. It only works within its authorisations and with the records made available to it. The point is: if it's authorised to access it, it receives structured relationships instead of a random pile of files.

Scene 5 · What improves for me

Linked records make my support more precise and more controllable.

1Focus

I can prioritise the records of a specific case and its relationships, instead of searching broadly and imprecisely.

2Currency

I can distinguish final and current versions from drafts, predecessors, and discarded states.

3Provenance

I can indicate which records, sources, and accountable statements a suggestion relies on.

4Clarifying questions

I can specifically flag missing relationships, unclear responsibilities, or contradictory states.

5Evidence

My support can itself be documented as a traceable work step.

The fifth point matters: AI-supported work can itself become part of the context. Not every assistance interaction must necessarily be a record. But where an AI contribution becomes professionally relevant, its role, the basis it used, and the human decision about it can be made traceable.

Scene 6 · Better assistance

I can support Ms. Roth — without taking over her responsibility.

She remains accountable for professional judgement, consensus, and finalisation.

Me as an LLM
work only with approved context

My support

I create a draft, reference the records used, flag a discrepant figure, and ask about a still-missing approval.

The right model of roles: AI accelerates research, structuring, and drafting. Humans retain responsibility, professional judgement, and the decision to finalise.

The example should not depict an automated government answer. Ms. Roth issues a clear instruction. The AI helps with drafting and surfacing uncertainties. Human responsibility is not delegated: professional review, team consensus, and finalisation remain with the accountable people.

Scene 7 · Augmentation needs guardrails

More context is only good if access, accountability, and evidence are governed.

Authorisation

I may only see and process records for which the mandate and access rights exist.

Human decision

I draft and support. An accountable person reviews, takes responsibility, and finalises.

Transparency

For relevant results, it should be visible which basis I used and what role I played.

RecordWeb is not a licence for autonomous decisions. It is a better foundation for traceable augmentation.
These guardrails are indispensable, especially in public administration. RecordWeb is no substitute for data protection, information security, access control, or clear accountability. But it can help implement these requirements more concretely when working with AI, because context, provenance, and responsibility are more structured.

Scene 8 · From answer to review

A good AI answer should be able to show its basis.

1

Mandate

An accountable person tasks the AI for a clear case or a clearly defined question.

2

Context

The AI receives authorised access to relevant, linked records and their status.

3

Suggestion

The AI produces a draft, names its basis, and flags uncertainties or gaps.

4

Decision

Humans review, add to, discard, or finalise. This decision remains attributable.

This is one possible control pattern, not a finished product promise. It makes clear what responsible AI use requires: a bounded mandate, controlled context, a traceable suggestion, and a human decision. RecordWeb can provide the information and evidence basis for this workflow.

Scene 9 · Traceability beyond internal work

When an authority makes information public, the verifiable holdings matter too.

Records that identifiably exist, are finalised, and can be resolved through their accountable context support verifiable public information provision.

For me as an LLM this means: I can not only summarise a text. I can — insofar as the information is public or authorised for access — point to clearly referenceable, accountable records.

The concrete link to personal data spaces or Solid pods is a possible later extension of this story. It is deliberately not a prerequisite for this presentation.
This slide picks up the reference to the principle of public access without making legal claims. RecordWeb can provide a technical foundation so that publicly relevant records verifiably exist and remain discoverable in their context. The Solid pod idea is mentioned only as later context, not as part of RWC/RWP or the PoC.

Scene 10 · AI is an accelerator

AI makes the weakness of disconnected information more visible — and the case for RecordWeb more urgent.

Without networked records

  • AI searches incomplete holdings
  • Important context stays in side channels
  • Versions and responsibility are hard to recognise
  • Humans must laboriously verify results

With RecordWeb as a foundation

  • AI can work within traceable cases
  • Records and relationships structure the context
  • Finalisation and provenance support control
  • Humans get better suggestions and better clarifying questions
The question isn't only: where do we deploy AI?
It's also: on which information architecture should AI work responsibly?
This closing argument targets leadership and architecture. AI is a strong driver for improving the information foundation. The investment isn't just an AI project. RecordWeb is the long-term architecture that can connect AI use, traceability, collaboration, and continuity.
