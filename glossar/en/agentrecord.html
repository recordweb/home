AgentRecord

Who is responsible?

Imagine a city approving a construction project. The building permit application itself is a case: it has a history made up of several documents (submission, query, amendment, decision), and that history can be proven without gaps.

But who decided? Which department is responsible? Who reviewed and approved the amendment? These people and units are not themselves cases with a version history – they are the responsible parties behind the documents.

RecordWeb therefore distinguishes two kinds of things, both identified through the same type of address (a DID):

  • Documents (InformationRecords) – the building permit application, the decision, every version of it. These things have a traceable history.
  • Responsible parties (AgentRecords) – the department, the case officer, the software that carried out an automated review. These things are actors, not cases. They appear within documents ("who approved this?"), but have no version history of their own in the RecordWeb sense.

This distinction matters so that a system resolving a RecordWeb reference immediately knows what it is dealing with: a traceable case, or the identity of a responsible party. It guards against an obvious misunderstanding – trying to retrieve the "version history" of a department or a person, something that simply does not exist in RecordWeb.
